The mission

Water stress is a systems problem. We are building a practical loop.

Every day, usable water is sent down the drain while homes, schools, and small communities face rising pressure on supply. AquaLoop makes reuse visible and manageable: a simple recovery system, clear measurements, and a path from prototype to real-world impact.

01

Make resilience accessible

Design around affordable parts and clear feedback so more people can participate in water stewardship.

02

Turn evidence into action

Track what is captured, treated, and reused instead of asking people to trust a black box.

03

Build for positive impact

Start with one loop, then share the learning that helps communities make better decisions together.

The product

From greywater to useful water.

AquaLoop connects physical recovery with a calm, understandable control layer. Four moments. One measurable cycle.

Collect

Capture suitable greywater at the source before it becomes waste.

Filter

Move water through a simple treatment path built for non-potable reuse.

Monitor

Use sensors and local control to make system state easy to understand.

Reuse

Put recovered water to practical, measured use where potable water is not required.
